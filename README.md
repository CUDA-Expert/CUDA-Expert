## About

I am a final year Artificial Intelligence student at the University of Prince
Mugrin in Madinah, graduating in December 2026.

My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, multimodal integrity,
spatial computing, and explainable retrieval. I care about the full system:
data quality, model behavior, evaluation, evidence, and the experience delivered
to the person using it.

I led the computer vision and multimodal integrity workstream for **IntiqAI**,
winner of **1st Place, Best Prototype** at the Makeen Annual Forum 2026.

## Selected Work

### IntiqAI

**AI recruitment integrity platform**

Identity verification, liveness and anti spoofing, deepfake screening, temporal
event logic, second voice analysis, and reviewer facing evidence reports.

My role covered the computer vision and multimodal integrity pipeline from media
preprocessing and model integration through event evidence and delivery.

`Senior capstone · Team project · University IP review`

### [Atlas Omega](https://github.com/CUDA-Expert/selected-ai-projects/tree/main/atlas-omega)

**Interactive spatial computing research build**

A hand tracking and WebGL environment evolving toward an uncertainty aware
planetary observatory. The repository preserves the working visual experiments
while separating scientific claims, provenance, and future architecture.

`Active research build`

### [Smart Self Checkout](https://github.com/CUDA-Expert/selected-ai-projects/tree/main/smart-cashier)

**Real time retail vision with YOLOv8**

A custom grocery detector connected to a Streamlit checkout flow with product
matching, billing, stock tracking, and inventory updates.

My contribution covered dataset construction, detector training and tuning, and
the computer vision checkout integration.

`University team project`

### [Explainable Quran and Hadith Retrieval](https://github.com/CUDA-Expert/selected-ai-projects/tree/main/quran-hadith-retrieval)

**Bilingual Arabic and English semantic search**

Hybrid retrieval across **40,098 verified records**, combining Arabic
normalization, multilingual embeddings, FAISS, TF IDF, metadata filters, and
explanations with source references.

My contribution focused on Arabic normalization, the hybrid retrieval layer, and
the evaluation harness.

`University team project · Active development`

### [Skin Lesion Malignancy Study](https://github.com/CUDA-Expert/selected-ai-projects/tree/main/skin-lesion-study)

**Learning under extreme class imbalance**

A study of preprocessing, CNN representations, classical classifiers, imbalance
handling, and threshold selection using the ISIC 2024 challenge setting.

My contribution focused on the CNN and image preprocessing track.

`University team project · Research archive`
